# Veyra AI Terms of Use — Draft

Last updated: 2026-08-16

This draft must be reviewed with the final legal entity, governing law, pricing and support details before publication.

## Service

Veyra AI provides AI-assisted creative tools for images, video, advertising, editing, enhancement, captions, brand workflows and related features. AI results may be imperfect and users should review outputs before commercial or public use.

## Accounts and credits

Users are responsible for account activity. Credits are digital service units and are not cash or cryptocurrency. Purchased, subscription and promotional credits may be tracked separately. Generation costs are shown before submission where practical. Failed eligible generation jobs may receive automatic credit refunds according to service rules.

## Acceptable use

Users may not use Veyra AI to create or distribute illegal material, sexual content involving minors, non-consensual sexual content, violent extremist propaganda, fraudulent impersonation, harmful malware instructions, or content that violates applicable rights or platform policies.

## User content

Users retain rights they hold in uploaded materials. Users must have the necessary rights and permissions to upload reference images, trademarks, music, voices, likenesses and other materials. Veyra AI may process the content only as necessary to provide, secure and improve the requested service as described in the Privacy Policy.

## AI output reporting

Veyra AI provides an in-product mechanism to report unsafe, infringing or otherwise problematic AI output. Reported content may be reviewed and action may be taken against accounts that repeatedly misuse the service.

## Purchases and subscriptions

Android digital purchases are processed through Google Play Billing and iOS purchases through Apple In-App Purchase where required by store rules. Billing, renewals, cancellations and refunds are also subject to the applicable store terms.

## Availability

AI providers, internet access and third-party services may occasionally be unavailable. Veyra AI does not guarantee uninterrupted generation. The service may route a request to another supported provider when configured.

## Account deletion

Users may request deletion inside the app and through a public account-deletion page. Some transaction, security or legal records may be retained where required by law or fraud-prevention obligations.
