## Review path

1. Sign in with the review account.
2. Open **Create / Studio**.
3. Enter a safe prompt such as: `A cinematic product commercial for a blue water bottle on a clean studio background.`
4. Verify credit estimate appears before generation.
5. Submit the generation.
6. Open **Projects** and verify queued/processing/completed status.
7. Use the **Report** action on a generated result to verify the safety-report path.
8. Open **Profile > Language** and change language.
9. Open **Privacy / Terms / Account deletion** links.
10. Billing products can be reviewed using store sandbox/test purchase accounts once configured.
