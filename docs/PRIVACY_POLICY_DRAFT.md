# Veyra AI Privacy Policy — Draft

Last updated: 2026-08-16

Veyra AI provides AI-assisted image, video, advertising and creative tools. This draft must be reviewed with final company/contact details before store publication.

## Data we process

- Account identifiers such as email, authentication subject and subscription plan.
- Prompts, project metadata and generation settings supplied by the user.
- Images, video, audio or reference files uploaded for an AI task.
- Generated outputs and generation history while retained by the user.
- Purchase identifiers and store transaction tokens used for server-side verification. Full card details are never stored by Veyra AI.
- Security signals, device/app integrity results, IP-derived abuse-prevention signals and server logs.
- Crash/performance diagnostics when production analytics is enabled.

## Why we process data

We process data to provide AI generation, maintain accounts and credits, verify purchases, prevent abuse and fraud, provide support, improve reliability, enforce safety rules and comply with legal obligations.

## AI providers

Generation inputs may be sent to configured AI providers solely to perform the requested operation. Provider API credentials remain server-side. The production version must list the actual providers used and link to their applicable privacy terms.

## Retention and deletion

Users must be able to delete projects and request account deletion from inside the app and through a public deletion URL. Account deletion removes or anonymizes account-linked prompts, references and generated outputs subject to legal, security and accounting retention requirements.

## Payments

Android digital purchases are processed through Google Play Billing and iOS digital purchases through Apple In-App Purchase. Store receipts/tokens are verified by the Veyra backend before credits or subscription entitlements are granted.

## Advertising

Free tiers may display ads. Development builds use official test ad identifiers. Production advertising identifiers and consent behavior must match the final AdMob configuration and regional consent requirements.

## Security

Secrets are never embedded in the mobile application. Sensitive provider, store and admin credentials are stored only in server-side secret storage. Critical generation and purchase actions may use platform integrity checks.
